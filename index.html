Elizabeth S. Forbes

Ecologist studying ecosystem responses to environmental change.

I'm a researcher interested in how animal communities and the ecosystems in which they are found respond to disturbance: species loss or introduction, management change, insect outbreak or fire, and harvesting of resources. I am experienced in field and lab-based inquiry, as well as quantitative methods - network analyses, hierarchical modeling, spatial and time series analyses. My work spans biomes, from rangelands to atolls, temperate and boreal forests, rocky intertidal zones, coastal saltmarshes, and agricultural farmscapes. I'm the Research Director at Hudson Carbon, where I lead a research program on regenerative agriculture and its effects on ecosystem services: carbon storage, soil health, biodiversity, and livelihood support.

My work combines long-term field observation, experimentation, and modeling. I care about open science, interdisciplinary education, compassionate mentorship, and bringing ecological research into public conversations.
